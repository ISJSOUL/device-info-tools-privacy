Device Info & Tools

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026

Device Info & Tools reads information about your phone and shows it to you. That information stays on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing about your device is uploaded to us — because there is nowhere for it to be uploaded to.

This policy is specific rather than general: below you will find the exact permissions the app requests, what each one is for, and every internet address the app is capable of contacting.

What stays on your device

Everything the app reads about your hardware is processed locally and never transmitted: processor, memory, storage, battery, temperatures, display, cameras, sensors, network configuration, installed applications, and the results of every hardware test.

Reports you export as text or PDF are written to your device's own storage. They are shared only if you choose to share them, with whichever app you choose.

Every internet address the app can contact

The app makes no background network requests. Each address below is contacted only when you open the feature that uses it.

  • speed.cloudflare.com Download and upload speed measurement, only when you tap Run Speed Test
  • api.ipify.org Returns your public IP address for the Network screen
  • ipapi.co Returns your public IP with the approximate city and internet provider that address is registered to
  • whois.iana.org WHOIS registry lookup, only for a domain name you type in yourself
  • google.com A connection test used to measure round-trip latency
  • any host you enter The DNS lookup, port scanner, and ping tools connect to whatever address you type into them
  • Google advertising servers Delivering the banner advertisements described below
On IP address lookups. Any service that tells you your public IP address necessarily sees that address, and ipapi.co also returns the approximate city and provider it is registered to. This is how the feature works, not something happening behind it. If you would rather not contact those services, avoid the Public IP card on the Network screen.

Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google collects your device's advertising ID along with device and network information including your IP address. This is Google's collection, governed by their policies, not ours — we receive no personal information from it.

  • Google's handling of this data is described in their Privacy & Terms.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before serving personalised advertising, and you can change that choice at any time from More → Ad privacy options inside the app.

Children

This app is a technical utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

There is no account to close and no data held on any server we operate. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, which is limited to your theme preference and your advertising consent choice. To clear the advertising ID Google holds, use the Android setting linked above.
